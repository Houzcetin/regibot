# Source conflicts

Authority order used by RegiBot: **Regulation** (Ön Lisans ve Lisans Eğitim-Öğretim ve Sınav Yönetmeliği) **> Student Affairs pages** (oim.yasar.edu.tr) **> Student Handbook** (Öğrenci El Kitabı 2025, Rev. 12).
When sources disagree, the bot follows the regulation and mentions the difference.

Checked topics: course registration, add/drop, withdrawal, ECTS load, attendance, repeats, grades and grade appeals, tuition, leave of absence, deregistration, duration of study, graduation.

The 3-day limit is the same. |
| 3 | Deregistration (kayıt sildirme) | *Kayıt Sildirme*: "Öğrenci İşleri Müdürlüğüne başvurulması yeterlidir." | **Madde 37(1)**: the application must be **written and made in person** ("yazılı olarak kişisel başvurmaları koşuluyla"). No tuition refund in either text. | Regulation: written, in-person application. |

## Consistent (no conflict found)
- Attendance: 70% of theory and 80% of practical hours unless the syllabus says otherwise. Handbook *Derslere Devam Zorunluluğu* matches **Madde 29(2)** word for word.
- Duration of study: 4 years normal and 7 years maximum for bachelor's; preparatory class max 2 years. Handbook *Öğrenim Süresi* matches **Madde 7(1)–(2)**. The handbook adds the extra-exam rights from Law 2547 Art. 44, which Madde 7(7) refers to.
- Tuition must be paid before registration completes (handbook *Öğrenim Bedeli*, **Madde 16 / 18**).
- No refund on deregistration (handbook, **Madde 37**).
- Leave of absence: handbook *Kayıt Dondurma* defers to **Madde 33(3)**.

## Student Affairs pages vs regulation (more specific, not contradictory)
- ECTS load: the regulation (**Madde 22–24**) sets 20–40 credits, max 40 for successful students (GPA ≥ 2.00) and max 32 for probation (GPA ≤ 1.99), applied from the end of the 2nd semester. Student Affairs adds that **all students take 30 ECTS in their first two semesters**, and that lateral/vertical transfer students get 30 ECTS in their first two semesters (up to 40 with advisor approval during add/drop). The bot gives the regulation rule and the Student Affairs detail together.
